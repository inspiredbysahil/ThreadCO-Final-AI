Privacy Policy

Last updated: Client to confirm

1. Overview

ThreadCo ("we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website, browse our drops, or make a purchase.

2. Information We Collect

When you place an order or contact us, we collect:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, and 10-digit Indian phone number.
  • Shipping Information: Physical delivery address, city, state, and PIN code.
  • Transaction Details: Items copped, sizes selected, order dates, and total amount.
  • Device & Browsing Data: Browser type, IP address, and cookie identifiers for session persistence.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process, fulfill, and ship your orders.
  • To send tracking updates and order confirmation notices.
  • To notify you 24 hours early regarding upcoming Friday drops (if subscribed).
  • To resolve enquiries and return requests.

4. Data Retention & Security

We do not store complete payment card credentials on our servers. All online payment data is encrypted and handled directly by authorized payment gateways. Guest shopping cart data is persisted locally in your browser storage.

5. Contact Us

For questions regarding your privacy, contact our Data Protection Officer at: hello@thread.co
