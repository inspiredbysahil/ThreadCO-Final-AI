Returns & Refunds Policy

7-Day Hassle-Free Returns on All Drops

1. 7-Day Return Window

We want you to love your ThreadCo fit. If the sizing is not right or you change your mind, we offer Free Returns within 7 days of delivery, no questions asked.

2. Return Eligibility Criteria

To qualify for a return or exchange:

  • The garment must be unworn, unwashed, and undamaged.
  • Original brand tags and wash care labels must remain attached.
  • The piece must be returned in its original ThreadCo packaging.
  • Caps and headwear must be free from makeup, sweat, or perfume marks.

3. How to Initiate a Return

  1. Contact our support team at hello@thread.co or via our Contact Form with your Order ID.
  2. Our courier partner will arrange a doorstep pickup within 48 to 72 hours.
  3. Once the parcel arrives at our Delhi HQ and passes quality verification, your refund or exchange is processed.

4. Refund Processing

  • Prepaid Orders (UPI/Cards): Refund will be credited back to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders: Refunds are transferred directly to your bank account via NEFT/UPI once bank details are submitted securely.

5. Exchanges for Sizing

Due to the limited nature of our drops, size exchanges are subject to remaining inventory. If your desired size is sold out, a full refund will be issued.
