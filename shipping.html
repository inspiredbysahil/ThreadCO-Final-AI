Shipping & Delivery Policy

Standard Shipping Rules for India

1. Free Delivery Threshold

We provide FREE Standard Shipping on all orders of ₹499 or more. For orders under ₹499, a flat standard delivery fee of ₹79 applies at checkout.

2. Dispatch & Delivery Timelines

  • Fast 48-Hour Dispatch: All in-stock drop orders are inspected, packaged, and dispatched from our New Delhi warehouse within 48 hours of order confirmation.
  • Standard Delivery: Delivered within 3 to 5 business days across major Indian cities and metro areas.
  • Express Delivery: 1 to 2 business days delivery available for select metro pin codes.

3. Order Tracking

As soon as your package is picked up by our courier partners (Delhivery, BlueDart, or Xpressbees), you will receive a tracking link via SMS and email.

4. Cash on Delivery (COD)

Cash on Delivery is supported across eligible Indian pin codes. Please ensure someone is available at the address to receive the parcel and provide exact cash or UPI payment.

5. International Shipping

ThreadCo currently ships exclusively within India. International shipping is planned for early 2026. Join our drop notification list to be notified first.

6. Damaged or Tampered Parcels

If you receive a package that looks damaged or opened, please take photos immediately and refuse delivery, or reach out to us at hello@thread.co within 24 hours.
